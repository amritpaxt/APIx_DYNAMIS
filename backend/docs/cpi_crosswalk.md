# CPI Crosswalk

APIx can run alongside manual air-fare collection as an experimental daily indicator. Its route weights map to DGCA passenger traffic, and its monthly aggregation can be compared to DGCA route-level published averages before any policy use.

The recommended transition is parallel collection, documented bias analysis by route and lead-time window, then a reviewed bridge series. APIx does not claim to replace a published CPI component without NSO methodological approval.
